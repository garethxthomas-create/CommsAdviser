How this works

The Bank (home)

Everything the Adviser knows about you, grouped in collapsible sections: Stories & evidence (what happened), Doctrine (how you work), Library (imported clippings and research), and Working documents (prep packs, reviews, debriefs). A Recent row appears once the bank is big enough to need one. Search covers titles, tags, and full text, and searching flattens everything, archived included, into one result list. The tag row shows your ten most-used tags; the rest sit behind the expander. Tap any card to read it.

Ask

The box at the top of the Bank. Ask questions in plain language, "what do I have on measurement?", "which stories show budget management?", and the Adviser answers from the full contents of the bank, citing entries by name and quoting exact numbers. It also tells you plainly when something isn't captured yet, and whether it's waiting on your backlog. Follow-up questions continue the conversation. Search below it stays for when you know the exact word you're looking for.

Capture

An interview about your past work. The Adviser proposes what to capture next from your story backlog and coverage gaps, then digs for the texture polished versions leave out: the people, the decisions, the numbers, the uncomfortable bit. Ends with a story entry saved to the bank, complete with a cue card: a glanceable quick-reference shown at the top of the entry and included in PDF exports. Cue cards are editable on any entry, so cards you already have can be pasted straight in.

Codify

The same interview pointed at your methods instead of your past. Produces doctrine: a principle, a playbook, a lesson, or a decision-journal entry. Doctrine is what Challenge mode thinks with, so every Codify session makes the Adviser more you. The eleven seeded entries are drafts; refining them here is how they become yours.

Challenge

Bring live work: a brief, a plan, a draft, an interview answer, a negotiation. The Adviser pressure-tests it one question at a time, armed with your own doctrine, citing your own playbooks when your thinking drifts from them. Ends with a written review: strengths, gaps, unresolved decisions, and danger zones.

Prep

Paste a job description or describe a room you are walking into. The Adviser reads your entire bank, decodes the audience, maps your stories to the likely questions, interviews you to fill the critical gaps, and writes the full prep pack.

Import (the Library)

Feed the bank from outside, two ways. File: a screenshot, image, or PDF; the Adviser reads it and extracts the substance faithfully. Text or transcript: paste anything, an article, an email, a YouTube transcript (tap "Show transcript" under any video and copy), or drop in a Teams/Zoom transcript file. Save as a clipping (a caught moment) or as research (a substantive source, which gets deeper extraction: findings with exact numbers, methodology caveats, quotable lines). Add the source link so every entry can point home; the reader gets a Source button. Everything lands in the Library section and feeds Prep and Challenge sessions. The "save exactly as pasted" tickbox skips extraction for entries prepared elsewhere in the bank's format, including config entries (Kind: config), which live in the System section and tune the Adviser privately without ever entering bank retrieval.

Finish & write up

End every session with this button. The written artifact is the point of the session; one without it is unfinished. Save it to the bank, or copy and download it as markdown.

Tags

The retrieval system. Add new tags, rename one (updates every entry that uses it), or retire it from the suggestion list. A specific entry's tags are edited inside its viewer.

Backlog

The list Capture and Codify propose from: stories not yet captured and methods not yet codified. When you save a matching entry, the app offers to mark the item captured automatically; you can also adjust statuses, add items, or remove them here. Feed it whenever you think "I should write that up sometime."

Archive

For entries whose moment has passed, an old prep pack, a superseded review. Archiving (button in the reader) removes an entry from the home sections and from the Adviser's working context, but keeps it searchable and exportable. Nothing is lost; the home screen just stays honest about what's current.

Export all

Downloads the entire bank as a single markdown file. Do this after any session that adds something important; career evidence should never live in only one place.

Settings

Your keys, stored only in this browser and entered once per device: the Supabase URL and anon key (the database), your Anthropic API key (the intelligence; a session costs cents), and the model. Sign out lives here too.

Practical notes: an unfinished conversation shows as "Resume session" on the Bank screen. Sign-in lasts until you sign out on that device. If the database sits unused for a week or more, Supabase may pause it on the free plan; waking it is one click in their dashboard, and your Export all copies make even the worst case boring.
